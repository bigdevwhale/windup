## [1.1.3] - 2026-11-19

### Changed
- **"At the desk" is quiet again:** it now suggests only what you can do sitting or standing at your own desk without kit, a wall, a door, a towel or the floor. Squats, desk push-ups, chair dips, wall sits, wall angels, the doorway stretch and the towel movements moved to "By the kettle" and "On a call" (#5).
- **Today's score helps you play it:** in "Where are you?" the toy of the note playing now comes first whenever it has a movement for that place, marked "♪ today's score" (#3).

### Added
- Six quiet desk movements, so every toy, the fair ones too, can be wound at work: seated leg extension (frog), desk pull (acrobat), desk press (miner), nose and ear (clown), knee squeeze (skier) and fast feet (hare). 90 movements in all (#3).

### Fixed
- Ankle circles are ten each way, so the set is 20, not 10 (#4).

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-13

### Changed
- New art: every toy (with its paint job), the shelf, the workbench, the music box, the fair stall and the capsule machine are painted illustrations instead of pixel art, animated smoothly at 60 fps.
- A new app icon: the tin frog with its brass key.
- The interface is no longer pixel: the Nunito font, paper-and-brass cards with rivets, metal buttons, a new bottom bar with icons and a capsule badge.
- The music box: an inlaid wooden case, notes rising from the toys as they play, a round play button, a 14-day calendar strip and equal tiles with icons, records as spinning discs.
- The workshop is split into Capsules · Fair · Toys · Kit; the purse and the settings live in its top bar.
- The toy's card: the toy alive on the bench, its state in large colour, and the last winding, metal, sets and rust in clear rows.

### Added
- An evening theme (dark walnut and felt), following the phone or set in settings.
- Statistics: streak and record, this week against the last, evenness, totals, the last two weeks by day, the hours you move in, reps, holds and floors, each toy's windings, parts earned.
- A ✓ on the plate of every toy wound today, and "wound today at 14:20" on its card.
- A guide on every screen at first visit, brought back by "?" in the top bar or from settings.

### Fixed
- Back from a movement opened from a toy's list (or from "Where are you?") returns to that list instead of the shelf.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-23

### Added
- The shelf: nine pixel-art tin toys with their own wind-up motions, wound for two hours by a short set.
- "Where are you?": seven places, three suggestions, 84 movements with ladders, kit at home.
- The workbench: clicks, holds, pocket counting by the accelerometer; pins with gears, springs and overwinding.
- The music box: any day plays as a tune with a parade of the day's toys.
- The workshop: capsule machine, the acrobat's blueprint, paint jobs, rust.
- Today's score: four notes in the quarters of the day; a finished score wins a record (17 tunes) for the album.
- Alloys: brass, copper, silver, gold for every toy, earned with parts and sets done with that toy; somersaults, glints, gilt.
- The shelf parade for winding every toy in a day; jammed toys after long neglect, freed by three sets in a day.
- The weekend fair: six fair toys with 26 new movements, two on the stall each weekend in turn; the shelf grows a board for every three.
- Gentle reminders inside waking hours; EN and RU; demo build.
