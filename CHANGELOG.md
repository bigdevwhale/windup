# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- The shelf: nine pixel-art tin toys with their own wind-up motions, wound for two hours by a short set.
- "Where are you?": seven places, three suggestions, 58 movements with ladders, kit at home.
- The workbench: clicks, holds, pocket counting by the accelerometer; pins with gears, springs and overwinding.
- The music box: any day plays as a tune with a parade of the day's toys.
- The workshop: capsule machine, the acrobat's blueprint, paint jobs, rust.
- Today's score: four notes in the quarters of the day; a finished score wins a record (17 tunes) for the album.
- Alloys: brass, copper, silver, gold for every toy, earned with parts and sets done with that toy; somersaults, glints, gilt.
- The shelf parade for winding every toy in a day; jammed toys after long neglect, freed by three sets in a day.
- Gentle reminders inside waking hours; EN and RU; demo build.
