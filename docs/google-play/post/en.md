Windup is now on Google Play 🐸🔑

A little movement, all day long – like winding tin toys. Nine painted toys stand on a shelf by the lamp; a minute of squats, pushes or balance winds one for two hours, so the key wants turning often.

• "Where are you?" – at the desk, by the kettle, in a queue: three movements that fit, 90 in all, most with no kit
• Every rep is a click of the key; squats and jumps counted by the phone in a pocket
• The day plays back as a tune on a music box, with a record for an even day
• Recast toys from tin to gold, a weekend fair with new toys, statistics and an evening theme

Free, no ads, no account, no internet. Everything stays on your phone.

Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.windup
