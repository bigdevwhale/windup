We were taught that exercise is an hour a couple of times a week. The body prefers a little, all day long: squats while the kettle boils, a one-leg stand while brushing your teeth, the stairs instead of the lift. Windup turns that into a shelf of painted tin toys. A winding lasts two hours, so the key wants turning often – a minute or three at a time.

🐸 NINE TOYS, NINE KINDS OF MOVEMENT
The frog squats and jumps, the monkey pushes, the bear holds the core, the ballerina balances, the drummer keeps posture and walks, the bird hinges, the robot turns, the penguin works the feet – and the acrobat, built from a blueprint, pulls. A toy left alone stops; left for weeks, it rusts and tips over.

📍 WHERE ARE YOU?
At the desk, by the kettle, in a queue, brushing your teeth, on a call, outside or with kit: the shelf suggests three movements that fit, the toy of today's score and the stopped toys first. 90 movements, most with no equipment at all; the rest use a kettlebell, dumbbells, a band, a bar, a rope or a backpack.

🔑 EVERY REP IS A CLICK OF THE KEY
Tap for each rep, or let a hold tick by itself. Squats, jumps and marching can be counted by the phone in your pocket. Ladders make a movement harder step by step and climb by themselves when a set runs long.

🎶 THE DAY PLAYS AS A TUNE
Each set hammers a pin into today's music-box drum. Pins spread across the day pay extra gears and springs; a third winding inside half an hour overwinds the spring. In the music box any day plays back, an hour a second, each toy a note: an even day is a melody, a crammed evening is one chord.

🎼 TODAY'S SCORE
Every morning brings four notes, one toy for each quarter of your waking day. Play them all and the evening gives you a record for the album – 17 old tunes to collect.

🥇 TIN TO GOLD
Recast any toy in brass, copper, silver and gold. Each metal takes parts and sets done with that very toy, so no favourite gets there alone.

🎪 THE WEEKEND FAIR
On Saturdays and Sundays a striped stall opens with two of six fair toys – a skier, a hare, a diver, a miner, a clown and a sailor – each a new family of movements. The shelf grows into a cabinet.

📊 STATISTICS
Your streak and record, this week against the last, how evenly you move, the last two weeks by day, the hours you move in, and each toy's windings.

🔔 ONE QUIET REMINDER
When the toys stand still for a while in your waking hours, one notification asks for the key. Unanswered, they space out, and after three it is quiet till morning.

🌙 DAY AND EVENING
Paper and brass by day, dark walnut and felt by evening – following the phone or set by hand.

ENGLISH AND RUSSIAN
Every screen in both languages; switch in the settings.

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no analytics – and no internet at all. Everything stays on your phone. Free and open source.

Windup is not a medical app. Choose movements that suit your body and stop if anything hurts.
